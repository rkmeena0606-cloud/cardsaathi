How Credit Cards Build Your Score

✅ Pay full bill every month — creates perfect payment history (35% of score)
✅ Keep credit utilisation below 30% of total limit across all cards
✅ Keep your first card active — older accounts improve credit age (15% of score)
✅ Request limit increase every year — same spend + higher limit = lower utilisation
✅ Space out applications — don't apply for 2+ cards in the same month
✅ Set up autopay for minimum amount — prevents missed payments
⚠️ Never pay only minimum — interest compounds at 3.75%/month = 45% p.a.
⚠️ Never use >30% limit even for large purchases — pay mid-cycle if needed
